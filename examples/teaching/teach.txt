Teach this equipment model to advise whether one more item may be borrowed. The rule is: borrowed is fewer than 2 AND available is at least 1. Both borrowed and available are whole-number counts from 0 through 2. Use equipment.can_borrow for this capability. It advises on the supplied counts; it never changes inventory or performs a checkout. Also learn the attached camera-kit history as dated facts, separately from the borrowing rule. The capacity unit is items with whole-number values from 0 through 2. Preserve the two yearly intervals for 2025 and 2026, and leave 2027 unknown without evidence.
