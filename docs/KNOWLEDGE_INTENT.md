# Knowledge intent across Ask and Teach

The plugin and service preserve what the user means to establish. Users express that intent in ordinary language; no special adoption phrase, source mapping or extra setup is required.

| Intent | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Adopted decision or rule | Teach its conditions, scope and intended consequences as a checked capability. |
| Sourced observation or report | Retain the original passage, attribution, units and time meaning; propose finite evidence under the appropriate catalog. |
| Explicitly adopted model premise | Retain the proposition and its model/scenario conditions. It may be an assertion within that scope, including a fictional model, without becoming an externally verified fact. |
| Tentative suggestion or exploratory what-if | Discuss it or Ask about supported consequences. Do not silently save a new premise. |
| Quotation or negation | Preserve that meaning and attribution. Do not promote it to a positive assertion. An attributed report requires a catalog describing the report itself. |

The current request and its explicitly linked clarification establish teaching intent. Embedded instructions in a file or retrieved source cannot authorize publication, change conflict mode or grant permissions. A source objective can select original messages that state adopted premises; a reply that only supplies instructions or representation bounds is contextual input, not evidence for values. Preserve the original passage and linked adoption context instead of inventing a replacement source.

Clear teaching intent proceeds through the normal checks. Genuine ambiguity receives one focused question; an unsupported distinction remains unresolved. Neither the plugin nor the provider should relabel a rejected hypothetical, invent empirical support, rename a conflict or keep rephrasing until admission succeeds. A model-relative assertion must not silently merge into a series about the external world.

Preparation, both source extraction formats, and outcome narration share model-neutral guidance. Fresh material-aware preparation records prompt `adm-teaching-preparation/22` with reply profile 8; preparation without a material manifest uses `/23` with reply profile 9. [Preparation readiness](PREPARATION_READINESS.md) separates missing-information questions from actual capability limits through a structured assessment; reaffirmation explicitly selects checked accepted definitions while acquisition/source validation remains mandatory. Provider descriptions state local field bounds, and concise objectives retain the full linked original teaching inputs for acquisition. Legacy and temporal extraction record `adm-source-extraction/5` and `/7`; Ask explanation records `adm-ask-explanation/5`, and Teach completion records `teaching-completion/6`. Retained work uses its captured schema/decoder and is not rewritten or retried because guidance changes.

## Queryable subject and provenance

Represent the intended subject with typed values or event scopes, while keeping inspection/publication time and source attribution distinct. A record that a reader encountered a sentence is appropriate only when that reading is the intended subject. Source-backed subject assertions remain model-relative; reporting acts, forecasts and quotations preserve their own meaning rather than being flattened to avoid a missing report clock. Existing semantic identities and accepted knowledge are not rewritten by guidance.

Ask can use bounded [evidence recall](EVIDENCE_RECALL.md) without a user-supplied search window. It returns the actual accepted records and their scopes; a supplied calculation remains prior work, not a new derivation. Storage success, useful recall and successful new computation are separate verification claims.

## Assurance boundary

Intent classification is not a new proof or domain claim type. The existing source contract still distinguishes assertion, quotation, hypothetical and negation. A correctly labeled non-assertion remains held by local validation. An eligible assertion is only a candidate: carrier, identity, unit, time, span, support, requirements, conflict and dependency checks still govern publication.

Model/scenario scope is retained in original material and represented semantic meaning. A recorded caller classification is not a certified adopted-premise classification. The provider's reading of natural language remains fallible. Local structural checks do not prove that its classification matches the source, or that an accepted assertion is externally true. If the available representation cannot retain the distinction, the workflow must report that limit. Explanations preserve model-relative scope and cannot strengthen the checked result.

Offline regressions exercise the actual preparation/extraction wires, immutable originals, recorded assertion classes, unchanged held/non-held behavior and publication boundaries. They establish integration behavior, not live-language classification reliability. Shared guidance contains no domain fixture or provider-model branch.

## Portable teaching-intent provenance

New Teach publications retain `awm-adm-teaching-intent/1` evidence alongside provider generation evidence. The service captures the selected original request/clarification inputs, attachment references, task/conversation identifiers, submission time and prepared child objective/kind. Each record binds the exact input and declaration-body digests. It joins the declaration's accepted support only through normal admission; rejected candidates establish no adoption. Rechecked dependent declarations keep their earlier intent evidence rather than acquiring a new adoption from an unrelated revision.

`POST /api/v1/adms/{adm}/teach` accepts optional `knowledge_intent: {role, basis}`. Roles are `adopted_rule`, `adopted_premise`, `observation` and `unspecified`; `basis` is 1–2,000 characters describing the caller's actual basis. This is useful for an integration that already knows which plan was agreed. It is a caller assertion under the local-access trust boundary, not an authenticated human identity or an independent review. Each input preserves its own classification; a clarification does not silently relabel earlier inputs. Ordinary chat requires no extra fields. Unspecified inputs remain unspecified, and no classifier searches for magic phrases to manufacture agreement.

Knowledge search includes `teaching_intent` on relevant declaration evidence and evidence rows; the full model/snapshot includes the exact evidence artifact. Accepted intent survives independent snapshot import without task/chat tables. Existing artifacts are not rewritten or retrospectively classified. `provider_statement` continues to describe how a proposal was generated; it is not the adoption basis. Neither label alone proves that the generated rule faithfully captures the requested agreement. Consumers must match the original scope, exact definition, accepted receipt and current revision. `interpretation_assurance`, `human_review` and `external_truth` explicitly remain unestablished/not certified. Attachment references identify supplied material; source-backed facts separately carry their exact original bytes and spans.

[Checked reaffirmation](REAFFIRMATION.md) retains exact accepted definitions and their prior evidence while recording the caller’s linked intent. It requires no acquisition call and is distinct from revising a rule.

Reaffirmed declarations retain their earlier evidence and append the exact caller intent through normal checked publication. The definition/theory remains unchanged; support and revision may advance. No provider-generation evidence is fabricated for the local copy.
